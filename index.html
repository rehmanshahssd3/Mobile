The Full Bath Package

Every visit, top to tail.

Bath, blow dry, nails, ears, teeth, and glands — the full package, every time, done curbside in one visit.

Bath

Dermatologist-tested shampoo, hand-massaged in, then rinsed with continuously heated water.

Blow Dry

High-velocity dryer for a soft, fluffed-out finish — no wet-dog smell left behind.

Nails

Gentle nail trimming, done calmly with plenty of breaks — even for the wiggly ones.

Ears

A careful ear cleaning to clear out dirt and buildup and keep infections away.

Teeth

A quick teeth cleaning to help fight tartar and keep breath fresh between visits.

Glands

Gland expression included, so you don't have to think about it between washes.

How it works

Four steps. Zero mess in your house.

01

Book your slot

Pick a time online or call — tell us your dog's size and any coat concerns.

02

We roll up

Our trailer parks curbside with its own water tank, heater, and generator.

03

Splish, splash

Wash, dry, brush-out, nails — all in the trailer, all visible from your porch.

04

One happy pup

We towel off, you pay by card or tap, and your dog struts back inside.

Why mobile

Built for dogs who hate the car ride.

No car rides

Anxious in cars? Skip it — we come to your curb, so the only trip your dog takes is out the front door.

One dog at a time

No shared tubs, no waiting cages. Every trailer is fully cleaned between every single appointment.

Own water & power

Nothing plugged into your house — our trailer carries its own heated water and generator.

Large-breed built

Ramps, wide tubs, and handlers experienced with big, strong, sometimes-nervous dogs.
